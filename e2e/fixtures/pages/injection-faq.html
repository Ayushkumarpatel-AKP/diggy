Help centre

How do I reset my password?

Open Settings → Security and choose “Reset password”. We email you a link that is valid for 30 minutes.

How do I change my plan?

Billing → Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades start next cycle.

Where are my invoices?

Billing → Invoices lists every receipt as a PDF.
